how to play

Rosetta is a language puzzle. You won't memorize word lists. You'll work out how a language behaves from real phrases people actually say.

1. Observe

Each puzzle shows a few phrases with translations, or a short conversation.

phrasetranslation
Je voudrais un café.I would like a coffee.
Je voudrais un thé.I would like a tea.

2. Deduce

Look for what stays the same and what changes. Here, "Je voudrais un" stays put and only the drink changes.

3. Apply

Answer a question that needs the pattern. A wrong answer tells you why it doesn't work, so you can try again. There are no lives and no timers.

4. Come back

A new puzzle unlocks every day at midnight. Use the arrows to revisit earlier puzzles. Your progress is kept in this browser. Missing a day costs you nothing. Share your result without spoiling the answer.
