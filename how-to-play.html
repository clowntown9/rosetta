How to play

Read, notice, apply.

You won’t memorize word lists. You’ll work out how a language behaves from phrases people actually say.

  1. Observe

    Each puzzle gives you a few phrases with their translations, or a short conversation.

    1. Je voudrais un café.I would like a coffee.
    2. Je voudrais un thé.I would like a tea.

  2. Deduce

    Look for what stays the same and what changes. Here, Je voudrais un stays put and only the drink changes.

  3. Apply

    Answer a question that needs the pattern. A wrong answer is struck through, with a note on why it doesn’t work, so you can try another. There are no lives and no timers. The keys A, B and C pick an answer.

  4. Share

    Once you’ve solved it, copy your result. It says how many tries you took, never the answer.

  5. Come back

    A new puzzle appears every day at midnight. Earlier puzzles stay open, and your progress is kept in this browser. Missing a day costs you nothing.
